AffiliateBot

AffiliateBot scans the descriptions of the videos on your YouTube channel and finds affiliate links that no longer work (Amazon, ShareASale, Impact, Rakuten, CJ, ClickBank, Awin and more), so you can fix them before they cost you commissions.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google or create an account with your email.
  2. Enter your YouTube channel ID.
  3. We scan your videos and list every broken affiliate link.

Pricing

  • Free: scan the first 50 videos of your channel to see whether you have broken links.
  • Monthly: $14.99 per month. View every broken link and scan all your videos.
  • Yearly: $99.99 per year. Same as Monthly, billed once a year.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Cancel any time from your account; access continues until the end of the paid period. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.
