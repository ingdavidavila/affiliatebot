Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information AffiliateBot ("we", "us", operated by WorkingRobots Inc.) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address. If you sign up with an email and password, we store a salted, one-way hash of your password, never the password itself. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address from Google.
  • Channel information you provide: the YouTube channel ID you enter to be scanned.
  • Scan results: the IDs of videos and the affiliate link addresses we found to be broken.
  • Subscription information: your plan status, renewal date and the customer and subscription identifiers Stripe assigns to you.
  • Technical data: a sign-in token stored in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in.

We do not collect or store your card number. Payment details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and handled by Stripe.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: sign you in, scan the channel you enter and show results.
  • To manage billing and your subscription.
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account, billing or support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Google and YouTube

AffiliateBot uses YouTube API Services to read publicly available video descriptions for the channel you enter. By using the Service you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke AffiliateBot's access to your Google account at any time in your Google security settings.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe processes payments and subscriptions (Stripe Privacy Policy).
  • Google / YouTube provide sign-in and video data.
  • Our hosting and database providers store and process data on our behalf.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and scan data while your account is active. To delete your account and associated data, email support@affiliatesbot.com from the address on the account. Some records, such as billing records, may be kept as long as the law requires.

Security

The Service is served over HTTPS and passwords are stored only as salted hashes. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or cancel your subscription at any time, by contacting support@affiliatesbot.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor valid requests.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@affiliatesbot.com.
