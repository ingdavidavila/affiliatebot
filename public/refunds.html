Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: November 4, 2026

In short: cancel any time and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Payments are generally non-refundable, but if you were charged in error, contact us and we will review it.

Free scan before you pay

You can scan the first 50 videos of your channel for free to see whether you have broken links before subscribing.

How to cancel

  • Sign in, open the Account menu in the top-left corner and choose Cancel Subscription.
  • Or email support@affiliatesbot.com from the address on your account and we will cancel it for you.

Cancelling stops all future renewals. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period (the month or year you already paid for), and you will not be charged again.

Refunds

  • Monthly and yearly payments are non-refundable once a billing period has started, and we do not give partial refunds for unused time.
  • Charged in error? If you were billed twice, charged after cancelling, or see a charge you do not recognize, email support@affiliatesbot.com with the email on your account and the date and amount of the charge. We will review it and, where the charge was our error, refund it.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take several business days to show it.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically each month or year at the price shown when you subscribed, unless you cancel before the renewal date.

Disputes

Please contact us before filing a chargeback so we can resolve the problem quickly: support@affiliatesbot.com.
